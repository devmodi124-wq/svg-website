Before you choose

On the Ganesha question

None of these use Ganesha imagery. It would be meaningful for a family firm, but a deity on a cylinder cap, a delivery truck and a GST invoice is hard to retire later and awkward to place. Concept 04 gets at the same idea through geometry. Say the word and I will draw a fifth that goes there directly.

Judge the favicon column

Most of a small supplier's brand impressions are a 16px tab icon next to five competitors' tabs. Concept 01 wins that test outright. Concept 03 loses it unless we draw a simplified variant.

Nothing here is locked

Colour, weight and proportion are all still open. Pick a direction and I will refine it — different accent, heavier strokes, a stacked lockup for the letterhead, whatever it needs.

What happens next

Your pick becomes components/Logo.tsx, the favicon, the Open Graph image and the WhatsApp display picture. Then I build the home page around it.
